# Brag Plan: OptiGesture

## What is this app?
OptiGesture turns any standard laptop webcam into a zero-latency, high-precision spatial air mouse controller for Windows 10 & 11 without any wearable sensors or cloud servers.

## The angle
"The mousepad is dead. Your hand is the mouse." A sleek, cinematic hardware-software showcase demonstrating real-time computer vision, zero-drift pinch clicks, smart magnetic button snapping, and hands-free head nod scrolling.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big cinematic title drop against a dark cybernetic grid: "WHAT IF YOU NEVER TOUCHED YOUR MOUSE AGAIN?" followed by an instant snap to the live HUD reticle tracking an air gesture.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Zero-Drift Pinch Click**: Instant coordinate lock the millisecond thumb touches index, clicking with sub-millimeter precision.
- **Tab-Style Magnetic Snapping**: Pointer magnetically pulled to nearby Windows buttons with a glowing cyan focus frame.
- **Head Nod Scrolling**: Tilt head down/up and return to neutral for smooth vertical scrolling with zero false triggers.
- **5-Second Pause Clutch**: Hold back of hand to pause with a dynamic charging countdown bar.

## Outro / punchline
"100% Local. Zero Cloud. Just Vision."
Final logo card: **OptiGesture** - Spatial Air Mouse.

## User flow worth showing
Point index to aim -> Pinch to left-click -> Nod head to scroll -> Show back of hand to pause.
